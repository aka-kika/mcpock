mcpock 1.12.1. For macOS 26 or newer on Apple Silicon. Update from the app, or download it from mcpock.com.

Fixes in 1.12.1
- **The usage note no longer covers your list:** the one-time note about call counts now sits under the list, and the list makes room for it. Nothing shows through it, in light or dark.
- **Grok Bot shows up as soon as it is installed:** it is listed on the Agents tab and in Settings > Agents even before it has any servers, with "No servers yet". Grok Bot's servers live in your xAI account and run in xAI's cloud, so mcpock lists them and never starts them.
