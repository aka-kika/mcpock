mcpock 1.12.0. For macOS 26 or newer on Apple Silicon. Update from the app, or download it from mcpock.com.

New in 1.12.0
- **Lost in a session:** when a server drops inside a Claude Code session that is still open, its row says "Lost in 1 open Claude Code session: run /mcp there", even while mcpock's own check reaches it. The card names the session's folder and when.
- **Grok Bot:** the servers in Grok Bot's settings show under Grok Bot. They run in xAI's cloud, not on your Mac, so mcpock lists them and never starts them; the row says so.
- **Antigravity:** mcpock reads Antigravity's servers from `~/.gemini/config/mcp_config.json`, and Settings > Connect has its row. Remote servers written as `serverUrl` (Antigravity, Windsurf) are read too.
- **Your own shortcut:** pick the keys that open the panel in Settings > General. Reset brings back Control-Option-M.

Lighter on your Mac
- **Every 15 minutes:** mcpock checks every 15 minutes instead of every minute. If you picked an interval yourself in Settings, yours stays.
- **Less reading:** a check reads only the config files mcpock already knows, and reads one again only when it changed. The full look through your config folders runs at launch and when you press Refresh.

Good to know
- **Usage counts:** a note in the panel says, once, that the call counts beside servers come from your agents' chat history on this Mac. Turn Off there, or in Settings > General, stops it.
