mcpock 1.16.1. For macOS 26 or newer on Apple Silicon. Update from the app, or download it from mcpock.com.

New in 1.16.1
- **Easier to read in light mode:** warning words ("The network connection was lost.", "Slow", "1 needs you", the lines in "Why it is slow") are a calm dark grey instead of amber, and the dot, the badge's tint and the bar keep their colour. Every grey is darker too: dates, file paths, "+3 more", "All fine". Measured on the glass, they now read at least 4.5 to 1 (they were 1.8 to 3.8).
- **Dark mode:** unchanged, but for the faintest grey ("No tools reported", the footer, dates), which is now as readable as the others.
