mcpock 1.12.2. For macOS 26 or newer on Apple Silicon. Update from the app, or download it from mcpock.com.

New in 1.12.2
- **Grok Bot has its own icon:** Grok Bot's rows on the Agents tab and in Settings > Agents show a mark drawn after Grok Bot's own app icon, in the same quiet one-color style as the other agents, instead of the letters "GB".
- **aka asks for access:** if aka asks for access before it lists its servers (its coming security update does), aka shows as "Needs access" on the Agents tab and in Settings > Agents, calmly, instead of quietly showing no aka servers.

Good to know
- **Clearer words:** the README and mcpock.com now say that secrets are masked, that the Agents widget shows up to five agents (the ones that need you first), and that updates are signed and asked about first.
